Vidyarthi Sahayyak Samiti

Empowering Rural Students Since 1955

Vidyarthi Sahayyak Samiti is an organisation which has transformed the lives of thousands of students from rural areas. Vidyarthi Sahayyak Samiti is a registered charitable trust under Maharashtra Public Trust Act 1950 with Charity Commissioner Office, Pune, having Registration No. E-219 (Pune).

Senior mathematician Dr. Achyutrao Apte, freedom fighter Haribhau Phatak, Smt. Sumitratai Kerkar and others started the work in 1955 with the objective of arranging lodging and boarding facilities at a very affordable cost in Pune, to boys from economically weaker sections.

Now Samiti has 8 hostels in Pune and 2 in Ahilyanagar where in all 1377 boys and girls are taking advantage of these facilities. Samiti is not merely a lodging and boarding trust. Apart from offering lodging and boarding facilities at concessional rates, more importance is accorded to the development of personality. Caste, creed, religion have no place in Samiti.

HISTORY

The idea of helping students took root in the mind of Dr. Achyut Shankar Apte in 1955. While pursuing education abroad, he observed that local citizens and the government helped needy students.

On returning to India, he shared the idea with like-minded persons and began by arranging meals for 5 students coming to Pune for education.
